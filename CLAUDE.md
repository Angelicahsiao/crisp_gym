For the first question I ask always:
- Prepare a plan of potential changes
- DO NOT CHANGE ANYTHING EXCEPT SPECIFICALLY SPECIFIED
- Ask questions on how to proceed always

Read GUIDANCE.md first. It says how to work in this repository, which parts of
HANDOFF.md matter for which task, what must not break, and what to do next.

Before working on the UMI handheld pipeline, pose representations, recording,
training, or model serving: READ HANDOFF.md in the repo root. It documents the
data conventions (rot6d, absolute-on-disk / relative-at-train), invariants,
verification tests, known bugs, and the agreed roadmap.
